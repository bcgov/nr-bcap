{% trans "What are Archaeology Sites?" %}

{% blocktrans %} Archaeological sites are the physical evidence of where and how people lived in the past. Sites in B.C. date from recent times to 14,000+ years ago, and are an irreplaceable part of B.C. history. They are investigated and protected for their historical, cultural, scientific and educational value to the general public, local communities and First Nations, and examples of archaeological sites include: {% endblocktrans %}

  • {% trans "Remains of ancient villages and cemeteries" %}
  • {% trans "Hunting and food processing sites, such as fishing weirs" %}
  • {% trans "Stone tool manufacturing areas" %}
  • {% trans "Culturally-modified trees" %}
  • {% trans "Ancient rock art" %}
  • {% trans "Shell middens" %}

{% trans "What is the BC Archaeology Portal?" %}

{% blocktrans %} The BC Archaeology Portal (BCAP) is where provincial citizens, consulting archaeologists, First Nations, proponents, and regulators come together to steward the archaeological record of BC. It is used to apply for permits, manage issued permits, submit archaeolgical site information, and to request information about a property or area of interest. Certain activities, like requesting information about an area of interest can be done without logging into the system. Permit and site work however, require you to obtain login access from the archaeology branch. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} The BCAP is a subset of the Provincial Heritage Register of historic and archaeological sites that exists as a statutory requirement of the {% endblocktrans %} {% trans "Heritage Conservation Act." %} Archaeological and pre-1846 sites are not included on the BCAP. Contact the {% trans "Archaeology Branch" %} {% trans "for information about archaeology in BC." %}

{% trans "Federal Government Historic Places" %}

{% blocktrans %} The federal government has responsibility for national-level formal recognition. National Historic Sites and other federal recognitions are not officially part of the BC Archaeology Portal but are noted in the system. {% endblocktrans %}
